Take Action

Spread Awareness

  • Share educational information about the Malayan Tiger on social media.
  • Teach friends and family about the importance of protecting endangered animals.
  • Create posters, videos or presentations to educate others about tiger conservation.
  • Encourage schools to organise wildlife awareness campaigns.

Say No to Illegal Wildlife Trade

  • Never purchase products made from tiger body parts or other endangered animals.
  • Avoid supporting businesses involved in illegal wildlife trading.
  • Report suspected illegal wildlife activities to the relevant authorities with the help of a trusted adult.

Make Responsible Choices

  • Choose products from companies that demonstrate responsible environmental practices.
  • Avoid supporting activities that contribute to deforestation.
  • Learn about the environmental impact of everyday purchases.
  • Encourage family members and friends to make environmentally friendly decisions.

Protect the Environment

  • Reduce, reuse and recycle paper to help reduce pressure on forest resources.
  • Avoid littering and participate in community clean-up activities.
  • Plant trees and support environmental conservation programmes.
  • Support efforts to protect Malaysia's forests and the Central Forest Spine.

Support Wildlife Conservation

  • Donate to trusted wildlife conservation organisations.
  • Participate in wildlife protection campaigns and fundraising events.
  • Volunteer in suitable environmental programmes.
  • Support organisations such as WWF-Malaysia and the Malaysian Conservation Alliance for Tigers (MYCAT).

Be a Responsible Wildlife Tourist

  • Visit ethical wildlife conservation centres that prioritise animal welfare.
  • Never support attractions that allow visitors to touch, ride or mistreat wild animals.
  • Respect wildlife by observing animals from a safe distance without disturbing their natural behaviour.
  • Share your experiences to encourage others to support ethical wildlife tourism.

Remember: Protecting the Malayan Tiger is everyone's responsibility. Every individual has the power to contribute towards a healthier environment, a stronger ecosystem and a future where these magnificent animals can survive in the wild!
